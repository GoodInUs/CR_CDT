Cognitive Urban Twin Ontology — v0.3.1 WebProtégé-Clean

Changes from v0.3
-----------------
1. Removed four OWL Restriction nodes attached to DecisionContext that WebProtégé
   rendered as blank/UUID parent classes.
2. Preserved all named classes, object properties, datatype properties,
   inverse properties, scenario individuals, evidence objects, decision context,
   candidate actions, recommendation links and explanation/assurance links.
3. Moved DecisionContext minimum-cardinality requirements into SHACL:
   - at least one DecisionEvidence
   - at least one affected DataCentre
   - at least one ImpactForecast
   - at least one CandidateAction
4. Added SHACL checks for DecisionRisk, DecisionStatus, decisionUrgency and decisionWindow.
5. Re-ran the 12 v0.3 reasoning checks.

Rationale
---------
OWL is retained for open-world semantic meaning and inference.
SHACL is used for closed-world completeness/validation requirements.
This gives a cleaner WebProtégé class hierarchy and a stronger modelling separation.
